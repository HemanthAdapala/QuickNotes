The Architecture of Stillness

The modern creative process is often interrupted by the very tools designed to facilitate it. Notifications, complex menus, and cluttered interfaces create a cognitive load that pulls the mind away from deep focus.

Gravity was conceived as an antidote to this digital friction. It is a space where the interface yields to the content. When you write, the UI evaporates. The margins expand, and the white space becomes a functional element of the workspace.

"Design is not just what it looks like and feels like. Design is how it works."

As we move towards more "tactile" digital experiences, we find that simplicity is not the absence of complexity, but the mastery of it. This editor uses progressive disclosure to keep you centered. Formatting tools only appear when you intend to use them.
